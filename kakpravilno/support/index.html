FAQ

How do I pass a level?
In a 10-question level you can make up to 2 mistakes; in a 5-question level, 1 mistake. If you make more, you can replay the level right away.

Is my progress saved?
Yes. The game remembers your level and question and continues from there next time. Progress is stored only on your phone.

How do I start over?
Delete the app and install it again from the App Store or Google Play. After you finish all levels, “Play again” also starts from level 1.

There is no sound.
Check the sound button in the top-right corner of the game, the phone volume and silent mode (the Ring/Silent switch on iPhone, sound mode on Android). If sound stops after you return to the game, tap the screen.

Do I need the internet?
No. The game works completely offline.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 15 or later and Android phones with Android 7.0 or later.
