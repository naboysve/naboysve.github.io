Privacy Policy

App: «Как правильно?» (“Kak pravilno?”) for iPhone
Effective date: November 19, 2026
Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works fully offline. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile app «Как правильно?» (the “App”), a Russian language quiz game. The App is developed and published by the developer shown as the seller on the App’s App Store page (“we”, “us”).

Contact for all privacy questions and requests: hyperpurple2020@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data or usage data through the App. Specifically, the App:

  • does not require registration or sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information;
  • does not contain advertising or advertising SDKs;
  • does not contain third-party analytics, crash reporting or any other third-party SDKs;
  • does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, health data or any other protected resources on your device;
  • does not offer in-app purchases and does not process any payment information;
  • does not connect to the internet. All questions, images, videos and sounds are included in the App.

The App’s privacy label on the App Store is “Data Not Collected”.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following on your device only:

  • the current level number, the current question number and whether the answers in the current level were right or wrong.

This information does not identify you, is never sent to us or anyone else, and we have no access to it. It is deleted when you delete the App. If iCloud Backup is turned on, it may be included in your device backup, as with any other app. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; we have no access to them.

The App uses haptic feedback (vibration) when you answer. No data is collected for this.

4. Data collected by Apple

The App is distributed through the App Store. Apple may collect information about your downloads and use of apps under the Apple Privacy Policy.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymized statistics (such as the number of installs) and crash reports. This information does not allow us to identify you. You can turn this setting off at any time.

5. Contacting support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem you reported. We do not use it for marketing, do not add you to any mailing list, and do not sell or share it.

We keep support emails for no longer than 12 months after the conversation ends, and we delete them earlier if you ask us to.

We use Gmail, a service of Google LLC, to receive email. Emails are stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and Google’s servers may be located outside your country. Google is bound by its own privacy commitments, which provide a level of protection at least equal to this policy. Sending us an email is voluntary; the legal basis for processing it is your consent and our legitimate interest in answering your request.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise share personal data with third parties, including advertisers, data brokers and analytics providers. We would disclose support correspondence only if required by law in response to a valid request from a public authority.

7. Children

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA), children under 16 (GDPR) or anyone else. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has emailed us, contact us and we will delete that correspondence.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress stays on your device until you delete the App.
  • Support emails are kept for no longer than 12 months after the conversation ends.
  • To delete all App data from your device, delete the App.
  • To have your support emails deleted, write to hyperpurple2020@gmail.com.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to data portability. These rights are provided, for example, by the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data”.

Because the App collects no data, the only personal data we may hold is support email you sent us. To exercise any of your rights or to withdraw consent, email hyperpurple2020@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Game progress is kept in the App’s private storage on your device and is protected by iOS. Support email is kept in a mailbox protected by a password and two-factor authentication.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect, and we will mention significant changes in the App Store release notes. If the App ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label first.
